<p><strong>NEU PC PLUS Programme (Subsidised Computer)</strong>
</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The IMDA NEU PC Plus Programme offers full-time students from low income
households the opportunity to own a brand new computer at an affordable
price. Parents can apply online at IDMA’s website using SingPass.</p>
<p>&nbsp;Please click&nbsp;<a href="http://www.digitalaccess.gov.sg/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">here</a>&nbsp;for more information.</p>
